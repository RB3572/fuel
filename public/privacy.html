Health Logger — Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

Health Logger is a bridge between Apple Health and a destination you choose. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What the app reads

With your explicit permission, granted per category in Apple's own Health permission screen, the app reads Health data: activity, heart, sleep, body measurements, vitals, mobility, mindfulness, and workouts, including workout routes where you allow it. You can change or revoke any of these at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Health Logger only ever reads from Apple Health. It never writes to it, and it never modifies or deletes your Health data.

Where your data goes

Only to the destination you configure. There are three, and you pick:

  • Fuel (fuel.rishib.com) — a private, single-user dashboard operated by the developer. Data sent here is stored so it can be shown back to you, and is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Your own server — any endpoint you enter. Data goes there and nowhere else. The developer never receives it and has no visibility into it.
  • A file on your device — the export feature writes your history to a file you control and hand to whatever app you choose. Nothing leaves the device unless you send it.

If you configure no destination, no health data leaves your phone.

What we do not do

  • No tracking, profiling, or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No sale or sharing of data with data brokers or any third party.
  • No use of your health data for training machine-learning models.

Storage and security

Your access token is stored in the iOS Keychain. Sync state is stored locally on the device. All network traffic to Fuel uses HTTPS; if you configure your own destination, its security is under your control.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes everything it stores on your device. Data already sent to Fuel can be deleted by asking at the contact address below; data sent to your own server is yours to delete directly. Deleting data in Apple Health does not by itself remove copies already synced, though the app does forward deletions it observes.

Children

Health Logger is not directed at children and collects nothing beyond the Health data you explicitly authorise.
